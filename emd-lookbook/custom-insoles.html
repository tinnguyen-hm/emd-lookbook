Custom Insoles

Provided by the Company
Eligible
Benefit Summary

Hike Medical custom insoles are now available through your employee benefits at exclusive pricing.

Foot pain rarely stops you all at once. It just talks you out of things, one at a time: the walk after dinner, the workout you keep meaning to restart, weekend plans that used to sound fun. Custom insoles are designed to your exact foot geometry, helping you offload the pressure of standing and walking. Your body can stop working around the problem and you can go back to doing all the things you love.

Coverage

Eligible employees have access to perfect-fitting insoles exclusively through the Company's partnership with Hike Medical.

If you're enrolled in the Company's medical plan (Blue Cross Blue Shield)Each year, your coverage includes 2 pairs of custom insoles at $120 each. Eligible dependents on the plan also have access to the same benefit. Depending on your deductible and coinsurance, you may pay some or all of that $120 per pair, with the rest billed to insurance. See the FAQ below for full details.

If you're not enrolled in the Company's medical planYou still have access to Hike custom insoles through the Company's partnership, at $120 per pair, a fraction of the $500 to $1,000 you'd pay through a specialist. Paid directly to Hike, with no claims or paperwork required.

How It Works

Plan members

  1. Verify. Enter your Blue Cross Blue Shield ID. Hike checks your plan in real time.
  2. Scan. Capture your feet in a 5-minute scan from your phone, whenever and wherever works best for you.
  3. Confirm. See your personalized cost estimate based on your plan, then a $1 hold is placed on your card while your claim processes. This is not a charge. Once Blue Cross Blue Shield finalizes your claim, typically within 7 days, you're charged only the confirmed amount.
  4. Delivered. Your custom insoles ship to your home in about 2 weeks. Hike builds and ships them while your claim processes in the background, so your order is never delayed.

Non-plan members

  1. Verify. Enter your employee ID.
  2. Scan. Capture your feet in a 5-minute scan from your phone, whenever and wherever works best for you.
  3. Pay. Pay $120 directly to Hike.
  4. Delivered. Your custom insoles ship to your home in about 2 weeks, built just for you.

FAQ

Who is eligible?All benefits-eligible employees at the Company, whether or not you're enrolled in the Company's Blue Cross Blue Shield medical plan. Eligible dependents on the plan are included too. Employees based outside the U.S., including Puerto Rico, are not eligible to participate.

How is this different from store-bought insoles?Drugstore inserts are molded once, for an average foot, and sold to millions of different feet. They flatten out fast and were never shaped for you in the first place.

How is this different from getting custom insoles through a specialist?Getting truly custom insoles through a specialist means $500 to $1,000 and months of waiting. Hike delivers the same clinical-grade result from a 5-minute scan, delivered to your door in about two weeks.

How does this work with my Blue Cross Blue Shield plan?Hike submits claims directly to Blue Cross Blue Shield. Like other covered services, your deductible and coinsurance may apply.

Will I know my cost before I order?Yes. Hike checks your plan in real time and shows you a cost estimate at checkout. A $1 hold is placed on your card, not a charge. The final amount is confirmed once Blue Cross Blue Shield processes the claim, typically within a week.

What determines what I pay?Two things: your deductible (what you pay before your plan starts contributing) and your coinsurance (the percentage you share after that). Here's how those apply to a $120 pair:

  • Already met your deductible and out-of-pocket max? You pay nothing. Your plan covers the full $120.
  • Met your deductible, but not your out-of-pocket max? You pay your coinsurance share. For example, at 20% coinsurance, you'd pay $24 and your plan covers the remaining $96.
  • Haven't met your deductible yet? You pay the full $120, and it counts toward your deductible.
  • Partway through your deductible? You pay what's left on your deductible, plus coinsurance on the rest. For example, with $100 left on your deductible: you'd pay that $100, plus 20% of the remaining $20 ($4), for $104 total. Your plan covers the last $16.

Will my order be delayed while my claim processes?No. Hike ships your insoles as planned while the claim processes in the background.

What if I'm not enrolled in the Company's medical plan?Custom insoles are still available to you directly through Hike, at $120. No paperwork.

Can my dependents use this benefit too?Yes, covered dependents under your Blue Cross Blue Shield plan are eligible.

Can I use my HSA?Yes. HSA funds typically cover custom insoles as an eligible medical expense. Confirm your plan's specifics with your HSA administrator.

Can I use my FSA?It depends on your FSA plan and how your administrator defines eligible expenses. Confirm with your FSA administrator before completing your purchase.
