Common questions

Everyone
How do I scan my feet? +
Use any mobile device to scan your feet, no app download required. Keep the foot centered and glide the camera from side to side. Watch the walkthrough below.
Who is eligible? +
All benefits-eligible employees at the Company, whether or not you're enrolled in a Blue Cross Blue Shield of Massachusetts medical plan through the Company. If you're enrolled in a BCBSMA plan through the Company, your eligible dependents are included too. If you're not enrolled in a BCBSMA plan through the Company, only you as the employee can access the benefit, not your dependents. Employees based outside the U.S., including Puerto Rico, are not eligible to participate.
How is this different from store-bought insoles? +
Drugstore inserts are molded once, for an average foot, and sold to millions of different feet. They flatten out fast and were never shaped for you in the first place.
How is this different from getting custom insoles through a specialist? +
Getting truly custom insoles through a specialist means $500 to $1,000 and months of waiting. Hike delivers the same clinical-grade result from a 5-minute scan, delivered to your door in about two weeks.
Can my dependents use this benefit too? +
For BCBSMA members: yes, covered dependents under your BCBSMA plan are eligible. For Not BCBSMA members: no, only eligible employees can access the benefit.
What if they don't fit? +
Each pair of Hike Medical custom insoles includes a Hike Fit Guarantee, which covers up to three rebuilds within 90 days of the original delivery date to fulfill the submitted order.
Can I use my HSA? +
Yes. HSA funds typically cover custom insoles as an eligible medical expense. Confirm your plan's specifics with your HSA administrator.
Can I use my FSA? +
It depends on your FSA plan and how your administrator defines eligible expenses. Confirm with your FSA administrator before completing your purchase.
If you're a BCBSMA member
How does this work with my BCBSMA plan? +
Hike submits claims directly to Blue Cross Blue Shield. Like other covered services, your deductible and coinsurance may apply.
Will I know my cost before I order? +
Hike checks your plan in real time and shows you a cost estimate based on your deductible, coinsurance and out-of-pocket maximum at checkout. A hold is placed on your card, not a charge. Your plan's $300 annual and $2,000 lifetime maximum for orthopedic shoes and foot orthotics factors into your final cost once Blue Cross Blue Shield processes your claim, typically within 7 days, so your final amount could differ from the estimate shown before you order.
What determines what I pay? +
Three things: your deductible (what you pay before your plan starts contributing), your coinsurance (the percentage you share after that), and your out-of-pocket maximum (the most you'll pay in a plan year before your plan covers the rest in full). Here's how those apply to a $120 pair:
  • Already met your deductible and out-of-pocket max? You pay nothing. Your plan covers the full $120.
  • Met your deductible, but not your out-of-pocket max? You pay your coinsurance share. For example, at 20% coinsurance, you'd pay $24 and your plan covers the remaining $96.
  • Haven't met your deductible yet? You pay the full $120, and it counts toward your deductible.
  • Partway through your deductible? You pay what's left on your deductible, plus coinsurance on the rest. For example, with $100 left on your deductible: you'd pay that $100, plus 20% of the remaining $20 ($4), for $104 total. Your plan covers the last $16.
What if I've already used my $300 annual or $2,000 lifetime orthotics maximum? +
These maximums apply to all orthopedic shoes and foot orthotics under your plan, not just Hike, so it's possible to reach either one before you order. To confirm your remaining balances towards the annual $300 and $2,000 lifetime max, reach out to Blue Cross Blue Shield Member Support. If you order through Hike and have already reached your annual or lifetime limits, you will be responsible to pay for any remaining cost identified after your claim is processed by Blue Cross Blue Shield.
Will my order be delayed while my claim processes? +
No. Hike ships your insoles as planned while the claim processes in the background.
