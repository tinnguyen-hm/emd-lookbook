Custom Insoles

Provided by Hike Medical

Drugstore inserts are molded once, for an average foot, and sold to millions of different feet. They flatten out fast and were never shaped for you in the first place. Getting truly custom insoles that are designed to your exact foot geometry through a specialist, helping you offload the pressure of standing and walking, means spending $500 to $1,000 and waiting months. Hike closes that gap by delivering the same clinical-grade custom insoles without having to jump through the hoops.

Coverage

Eligible employees have access to perfect-fitting insoles exclusively through the Company's partnership with Hike Medical.

If you're enrolled in the Company's medical plan (Blue Cross Blue Shield)

Each year, your coverage already includes $300 for orthopedic shoes and foot orthotics (up to a $2,000 lifetime maximum). Starting in 2027, you can also leverage this benefit with Hike Medical for up to 2 pairs of custom insoles at $120 each.

Eligible dependents on the plan also have access to the same benefit. Depending on your deductible, coinsurance and out-of-pocket maximum at the time of ordering, you may pay some or all of that $120 per pair, with the rest billed to insurance.

To confirm your remaining balances towards the annual $300 and $2,000 lifetime max, reach out to Blue Cross Blue Shield Member Support. If you order through Hike and have already reached your annual or lifetime limits, you will be responsible to pay for any remaining cost identified after your claim is processed by Blue Cross Blue Shield.

If you're not enrolled in the Company's medical plan

You still have access to Hike custom insoles through the Company's partnership, at $120 per pair, up to 2 pairs a year, a fraction of the $500 to $1,000 you'd pay through a specialist. Paid directly to Hike, with no claims or paperwork required.

Eligibility to receive Hike Custom Insoles is subject to the agreement between your organization and Hike Medical, including your current employment and/or membership status. Questions? Contact your benefits administrator or support@hikemedical.com.
